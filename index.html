FAQ

What would you like to know about Us

What happens after I buy?
You get instant access to your dashboard with CSV download option. Leads are delivered within 24 hours and you can start reaching out immediately.
Can I get sample leads before purchasing?
Yes, we offer a sample pack of 10 leads for first-time users at a nominal cost so you can verify quality before committing.
What makes these different from cold calling data?
Our leads are sourced from active social platforms where users have shown intent, filtered by niche and demographics. They're warm, not cold.
Is there a refund if leads are invalid?
We verify all leads before delivery. If invalid contacts exceed 10%, we offer instant replacement credits to your wallet.
Can I filter leads by location and age?
Yes, our platform supports filtering by city, state, age group, gender, and audience type to match your exact campaign needs.
