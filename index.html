Frequently Asked Questions

How do I download my leads?
Go to Downloads section, find your order and click the download button. You'll get a CSV file with all lead details.
What if leads are invalid?
If invalid leads exceed 10%, we offer instant replacement credits. Raise a ticket with proof.
How long until I receive leads?
Leads are delivered within 24 hours of payment confirmation. Most orders are fulfilled within 2-4 hours.
Can I get a refund?
Due to the digital nature of our product, we don't offer refunds. However, we provide replacement credits for invalid leads.
